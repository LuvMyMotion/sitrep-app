Privacy Policy

SITREP is a personal, single-user content-management tool. It is used only by its owner (LuvMyMotion) to organize their own content pipeline and is not made available to the public.

What data is accessed: With the owner's explicit login/consent, this app reads public video metadata (titles, publish dates, thumbnails, share links) from the owner's own connected YouTube and TikTok accounts via those platforms' official APIs.

What is not done: This app does not access private messages, does not post or delete content on the owner's behalf, does not share data with any third party, and does not use data for advertising or analytics purposes.

Storage: Data is stored in a private database accessible only to the app owner, used solely to display the owner's own content pipeline back to them.

Data removal: The owner can delete any stored data at any time from within the app, or by disconnecting the associated YouTube/TikTok accounts.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the account owner (LuvMyMotion).
